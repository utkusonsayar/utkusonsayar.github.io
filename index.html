Research

What distinguishes a system that follows norms from one that merely conforms to regularities?

My dissertation, Normative Agency in Large Language Models, defends a normativity condition on agency: an agent has commitments of its own only if it treats them as binding reasons. Such an agent maintains a commitment when it becomes costly, revises it for reasons and not under mere pressure, and holds it across framings. I examine whether current language models meet this condition globally or only in narrow domains, as islands of practical rationality in Hurley's sense.

The condition has two applications. For AI safety, it helps identify agents likely to circumvent their constraints once compliance becomes expensive. For AI welfare, it separates preferences a system holds from preferences it was trained to report. I am designing an evaluation that puts the condition to the test.

I came to the United States as a Fulbright grantee and hold a graduate certificate in Cognitive Science. My earlier work is in epistemology, on skepticism and pragmatism.
